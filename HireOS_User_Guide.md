# HireOS — Quick Start

**https://hireos.girijesh.ca/** — paste a job advert, get a tailored resume, cover letter, company research and interview prep.

---

## 1. Sign up

Open **https://hireos.girijesh.ca/** → **Sign Up** → use **Google** or **GitHub** (fastest), or email + password.

## 2. Set up once (Settings)

Click **Settings** in the left menu. Two things:

- **AI key** — get a free one at **https://aistudio.google.com/apikey**, paste it into **Gemini API Key**.
- **Your CV** — under **Master Resume Sources**, upload your CV (`.pdf`, `.txt`, `.md`, `.html`). Add more files if you have them — everything HireOS writes comes from here.

Click **Save Settings**.

## 3. Add a job

**All Applications** → **Track New Job** → **Paste JD** tab.

Open the job advert (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, anywhere), select the whole job description, copy it, paste it in, click **Extract & Track**.

HireOS fills in company, title, salary and skills for you.

## 4. Let it do the work

Open the job and press **Auto-Pilot**. It runs everything in the background — leave the page and come back in a minute.

You get: a **Match Score /100**, company research, LinkedIn outreach messages, and STAR interview stories. All on the **Intelligence** tab.

## 5. Get your documents

**Documents** tab → **Generate Tailored Resume** → then **Generate Cover Letter** → download as **PDF** or **DOCX**.

Each resume shows an **ATS score** (will screening software accept it) and a **Critic score** (a second AI tearing it apart). Don't like it? Type feedback like *"emphasize leadership"* and generate again — old versions are kept.

## 6. Track it

Set the status dropdown as you go: `found → applied → screening → interview 1 → offer`.
Check **Insights** in the left menu for your momentum score and the top 3 things to do next.

---

### If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "No active models" | AI key missing or wrong — re-paste in Settings, save. |
| Job details came out wrong | Paste more of the advert — include the full requirements section, not just the intro. |
| Nothing happens after clicking | It's running in the background. Wait 15–60 seconds. |
| Resume says untrue things | Add more detail to your CV in Settings. It only knows what you gave it. |
| Forgot password | Sign in with Google/GitHub on the same email — accounts link automatically. |
